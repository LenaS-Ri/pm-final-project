## Responses
- **Chosen path, which scenario are you committing to? (StreamLine or RouteLogic, or your own):** Riverty Case Study: BU Pay & Credit — Riverty's First Consumer Card
- **Strategic crisis, based on the brief, if the company does nothing for 12 months, what happens to its market position?:** If Riverty does nothing, its current gaps persist: no physical presence, underserved heavy users and weak brand pull. Meanwhile, Klarna and PayPal can deepen direct consumer relationships beyond checkout. Riverty risks remaining dependent on merchant checkout for consumer reach and missing a significant growth opportunity, while still protecting its merchant-first strategy.
- **Moment of misery, what is the user likely doing outside the product (Google, spreadsheets, a competitor) because your tool isn’t serving them?:** Lena values payment flexibility but has to piece it together across Klarna, PayPal and existing cards. Different balances, due dates and providers can make it harder to stay in control, especially when a return, repayment or purchase doesn't go as planned.
Hypothesis to validate: fragmentation and loss of control are painful enough to influence her choice of payment provider.
- **Problem hook, summarize the business risk and user pain into one urgent sentence.:** Riverty risks losing relevance beyond existing merchants' checkouts, while consumers like Lena lack one flexible payment relationship that keeps them in control, especially when things don't go as planned. 
- **Value proposition, based on the proposed initiative (Spotlight or Velocity), what is the new value and why is it urgent to launch now?:** The Riverty Card combines the reach of Mastercard with the flexibility of BNPL, giving Lena one place to see what she owes, control when she pays, and get support when things don't go as planned.
Why now: Riverty already has the Mastercard membership and issuing platform. The opportunity is to turn that capability into a differentiated consumer relationship before competitors further strengthen theirs.
