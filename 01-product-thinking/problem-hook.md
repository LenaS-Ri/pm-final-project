## Responses
- **Chosen path, which scenario are you committing to? (StreamLine or RouteLogic, or your own):** Riverty Case Study: BU Pay & Credit — Riverty's First Consumer Card
- **Strategic crisis, based on the brief, if the company does nothing for 12 months, what happens to its market position?:** Current model gaps will persist, i.e. still no physical presence, underserved heavy users, weak brand pull. At the same time, competitors such as Klarna and PayPal have already entered the card business and will seek their chance to pull users to them. Riverty remains only dependent on merchant checkout (even if merchant-first remains the core conscious strategy!) for consumer reach, while competitors such as Klarna and PayPal build broader, direct consumer relationships across online and offline spending. Overall, Riverty would loose potential opportunity bets and miss growth potentials and most probably growth targets by its shareholders.
- **Moment of misery, what is the user likely doing outside the product (Google, spreadsheets, a competitor) because your tool isn’t serving them?:** Lena switches between Klarna, PayPal and existing cards depending on where she shops, while managing different payment dates, balances and providers. She likely looses overview which also means loosing control for her, which seems to be a big value for her. When a purchase, return or repayment doesn't go as planned, staying in control becomes harder. Switching among different BNPL providers without really liking the product can be a misery. Riverty will not be placed as a good alternative to the user. Overall, user might even loose trust in BNPL products overall if all of them don't serve her needs.
- **Problem hook, summarize the business risk and user pain into one urgent sentence.:** Riverty risks loosing relevance beyond checkout, while consumers like Lena lack one flexible payment relationship that keeps them in control, especially when things don't go as planned.
- **Value proposition, based on the proposed initiative (Spotlight or Velocity), what is the new value and why is it urgent to launch now?:** One value to serve users like Lena (up for further research!) could be:
Lena likes the flexibility of BNPL, but using flexible payment options across purchases makes it harder to maintain a clear picture of what she owes and when money will leave her account. (assumed painpoint: control + broader accessibility of the same provider )
What if Lena could get the flexibility she values across everyday purchases by making a Riverty card usable everywhere, while always knowing exactly what she owes, when she'll pay, and how much she can safely spend?
--> The Riverty Card brings flexible payments into Lena's everyday spending - across existing Riverty merchants, giving her one clear view of what she owes, control over when she pays, and support when something goes wrong, wherever Mastercard is accepted. Combining high reach via mastercard with the control + flexibility she loves from BNPL afterwards.
