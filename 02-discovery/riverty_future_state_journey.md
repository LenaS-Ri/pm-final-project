# Future-State Journey: Lena with the Riverty Card

**Journey objective:** Move Lena from fragmented payment management to
**one flexible, controlled payment relationship**.

## 1. Choose & Pay

-   **User Action:** Uses Riverty Card anywhere Mastercard is accepted
    -\> avoids merchant-dependent BNPL availability.
-   **Internal State:** "I can use Riverty here." -\> reduces
    payment-method switching and decision effort.
-   **Pain Point Addressed:** Broad card acceptance -\> removes
    dependence on Riverty being offered at checkout.

↓

## 2. See & Understand

-   **User Action:** Opens Riverty overview -\> sees purchases, amounts
    and due dates in one place.
-   **Internal State:** "I know what I owe." -\> replaces uncertainty
    with a clear financial picture.
-   **Pain Point Addressed:** Unified overview -\> reduces scattered
    purchases, reminders and conflicting payment information.

↓

## 3. Control When to Pay

-   **User Action:** Reviews purchase and repayment options -\> chooses
    when money leaves her account.
-   **Internal State:** "I decide when I pay." -\> creates control
    instead of reacting to reminders.
-   **Pain Point Addressed:** Flexible repayment -\> supports cash-flow
    control while deciding what to keep or return.

↓

## 4. Resolve When Things Go Wrong

-   **User Action:** Flags return or payment issue -\> sees status and
    required next step.
-   **Internal State:** "I know what's happening." -\> reduces anxiety
    and manual chasing.
-   **Pain Point Addressed:** Clear issue status -\> reduces uncertainty
    when returns and payment obligations diverge.

## Competitive Advantages vs. Lena's Manual Workaround

1.  **Broad reach -\> One Riverty relationship across merchants and
    online/offline purchases.**
2.  **Unified overview -\> Replaces checking multiple providers, emails
    and payment reminders.**
3.  **Integrated control -\> Combines payment flexibility, visibility
    and issue handling in one experience.**

> **Scope note:** Automatic return reconciliation is not assumed. The
> journey uses "flag issue -\> see status and next step" because the
> available case material and simulated research do not establish that
> the MVP can automatically detect and pause merchant returns.
