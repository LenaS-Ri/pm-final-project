# Roadmap, PRD & Prototype (Module 4)

## Your strategic anchors
- **Persona (M2), who are you solving for?:** Lena, 32, Cologne. A salaried, mobile-first shopper with limited financial cushion who regularly uses BNPL and switches between Klarna, PayPal and Riverty depending on availability.
- **Primary success metric (M3), your leading indicator:** M6 Active Card Rate: ≥50% of activated cardholders complete at least 2 Riverty Card transactions in Month 6.
- **Moment of misery (M2), the specific friction blocking the goal:** When a return or payment doesn't go as planned, Lena cannot clearly see what she owes or when she needs to pay, forcing her to reconcile fragmented merchant and payment information herself.
- **Guardrail metric (M3), what must not drop or break:** Partner-merchant checkout conversion: must not decline by more than 1 percentage point versus baseline/control, protecting Riverty's merchant-first business.

## Scan the backlog & set a human baseline
- **My instinctive “quick wins” before touching the AI (2 to 3 feature IDs + why):** Full list:
| # | Feature | What it does | Origin |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Consumer Card Onboarding Journey** | Connects existing identity, agreements and card capabilities into a seamless consumer application and activation flow. | Product / UXR |
| 2 | **Virtual Card Provisioning** | Issues and activates the consumer Mastercard digitally after successful onboarding. | Product / Engineering |
| 3 | **Unified Card + BNPL Overview** | Integrates card purchases into Riverty's existing purchase overview alongside BNPL transactions. | UXR / Product |
| 4 | **Card Transaction Enrichment** | Makes raw card transactions understandable through clear merchant names, locations and purchase details. | UXR / Engineering |
| 5 | **Flexible Card Repayment** | Lets customers apply eligible Riverty flexible-payment options to card purchases. | UXR / Commercial |
| 6 | **Apple Pay / Google Pay** | Enables users to add the Riverty Card to their preferred mobile wallet. | UXR / Growth |
| 7 | **Merchant Rewards & Cashback** | Rewards card usage at Riverty merchants, reinforcing the merchant-first ecosystem. | Sales / Strategy |
| 8 | **Smart Spending Insights** | Categorises card spending and provides monthly summaries and spending insights. | UXR / Engagement |
| 9 | **Physical Card** | Gives customers a physical Mastercard for situations where mobile/virtual payment isn't available. | UXR / Marketing |
| 10 | **Premium Card Tier** | Adds enhanced benefits, rewards or services for highly engaged cardholders. | Commercial / Marketing |

--> my quick wins: 
* 1 --> re-use existing capabilities and build the baseline
*2 --> re-use what we have from b2b pilot and again baseline necessity
*3 --> without this: no value for Lena at all, also re-using app capabilities 

Quick wins is relative here. if we really start from scratch, we need to build a baseline first before adding smaller quick wins!
