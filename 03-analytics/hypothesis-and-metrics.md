## Pre-work · Hypothesis check
- **Role , who you are solving for (from M2):** Lena, 32, Cologne, is a salaried, mobile-first shopper with limited financial cushion who regularly uses BNPL for non-trivial purchases and switches between Klarna, PayPal and sometimes Riverty depending on what is available at checkout.
- **Goal , what this user is ultimately trying to achieve:** Stay in control of when her money leaves her account, with one clear view of what she owes, when it is due, and what requires her attention.
- **Friction / moment of misery , the specific pain blocking their goal:** When a return, refund or payment doesn't go as planned, Lena cannot easily tell what she actually owes or when she needs to pay, forcing her to choose between paying for something she returned or risking a late payment. This is especially enforced when she does not have one single payment provider across checkouts, but is forced to choose different ones because there is no single BNPL provider she can use everywhere.
- **Current workaround , the external tool or manual process they rely on (M2):** She manually pieces together information across Klarna, PayPal, Riverty, merchant return information, emails and reminders to understand her purchases, returns and upcoming payments.
- **Problem Hook , your one-sentence framing of the business crisis (M1):** Riverty risks losing relevance beyond existing merchants' checkouts, while consumers like Lena lack one flexible payment relationship that keeps them in control, especially when things don't go as planned.
- **Value Proposition , the outcome your initiative promised to deliver (M1):** The Riverty Card combines the reach of Mastercard with the flexibility of BNPL, giving Lena one place to see what she owes, control when she pays, and get support when things don't go as planned.

- **Persona , role, goal, and the friction you confirmed in the reconciliation steps:** Role: Lena, 32, Cologne, salaried mobile-first shopper with limited financial cushion and frequent BNPL usage.
Goal: Stay in control of when she pays and clearly understand what she owes across purchases.
Confirmed friction: When returns and payment obligations don't align, fragmented information leaves her unsure what she owes, when she needs to pay, and whether she risks a late fee.
- **Problem you are solving , one sentence describing the specific friction this initiative removes:** Remove the fragmented payment experience that forces Lena to manually reconcile purchases, returns, amounts due and payment dates across merchants and payment providers.
